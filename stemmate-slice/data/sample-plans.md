# Sample (synthetic) plans for the demo and for evaluators

All fictional. Type or paste these into the "Create a session plan" form.
No real people, learners, phone numbers or email addresses.

## Sample plan 1
- **Plan title:** Balloon rocket race, Grade 5
- **Linked activity:** Balloon rocket race
- **Duration:** 30
- **Steps:**
  ```
  Tie a string between two chairs
  Thread a straw onto the string
  Tape an inflated balloon to the straw
  Release the balloon and measure how far it travels
  ```
- **Materials:** balloons, string, straws, tape
- **Safety note:** Keep balloons away from faces. An adult handles scissors.

## Sample plan 2
- **Plan title:** Bottle water filter, Grade 7
- **Linked activity:** Bottle water filter
- **Duration:** 45
- **Steps:**
  ```
  Cut the bottom off a plastic bottle (adult does this)
  Add layers of cloth, sand and gravel
  Pour muddy water through the top
  Compare the water before and after
  ```
- **Materials:** plastic bottle, sand, gravel, cloth, muddy water
- **Safety note:** Do not drink the filtered water. Adult cuts the bottle.

## Sample plan 3
- **Plan title:** Paper bridge test, Grade 6
- **Linked activity:** Paper bridge strength test
- **Duration:** 40
- **Steps:**
  ```
  Place two stacks of books 20 cm apart
  Fold a sheet of paper to make a bridge
  Add coins one at a time until it bends
  Record how many coins it held
  ```
- **Materials:** A4 paper, tape, coins, books
- **Safety note:** Keep fingers clear of falling books.
